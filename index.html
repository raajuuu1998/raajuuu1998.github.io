I am an independent computational pathology researcher from India. My research focuses on developing machine-learning methods that extract clinically relevant information from histopathology whole-slide images, with particular emphasis on pathology foundation models, weakly supervised learning, biological representation learning, and robust biomarker and clinical outcome prediction.

My work investigates how morphological and spatial biological information can improve the generalization, interpretability, and clinical relevance of computational pathology systems across heterogeneous patient cohorts and disease settings.

Pathology Foundation Models

I study how pretrained pathology foundation models encode morphological and biological information in histopathology whole-slide images, with emphasis on clinically relevant representations and generalization across patient cohorts.

Weakly Supervised Learning for Whole-Slide Images

I develop multiple-instance learning approaches for learning from slide- and patient-level labels without dense region annotations, with particular interest in robust aggregation and interpretable tissue representations.

Biologically Informed Representation Learning

My work explores how spatial tumor-microenvironment information and biologically grounded descriptors can complement morphology learned by pathology foundation models.

Molecular Biomarker and Clinical Outcome Prediction

I investigate computational pathology methods for predicting molecular phenotypes and clinically relevant outcomes directly from routine H&E histology.
